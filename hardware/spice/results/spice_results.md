# SPICE verification of the analog chain (ngspice)

Generated by `hardware/spice/run_spice.py` from the component values in `hardware/gen/boards.py`. Op-amp: behavioural MCP6022 (A0 110 dB, GBW 10 MHz, 65 deg PM, 8.7 nV/rtHz, rail-to-rail). Amp: behavioural TPA6132A2 with a 0.9 V peak output clamp. Driver: a typical-class 40 mm Thiele-Small model, swept; replace with the chosen driver's measured parameters.

## 1. Mic front ends

| Channel | Gain (V/V) | -3 dB band | Noise, input-referred | Clips at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REF (outside) | 3.42 | 7.6 Hz - 9.5 kHz | 3.9 uV(A) = 23.8 dB(A) SPL | 31.9 Pa = 124.0 dB peak |
| ERR (in-cup) | 6.84 | 7.6 Hz - 9.5 kHz | 3.4 uV(A) = 22.6 dB(A) SPL | 15.9 Pa = 118.0 dB peak |

- Response matches the simulation's plant (`sim/plant.py`: 10 Hz high-pass, 3rd-order ~10 kHz anti-alias), so the ANC results stand on the real circuit.
- Electronics noise is about the mic's own self-noise (21 dB(A) SPL): fine for ANC at 100 dB(A) and for quiet-room dosimetry.
- **Finding - ear-overload cut-out cannot fire:** the in-cup channel saturates at 15.9 Pa, but the firmware trips at 22.5 Pa (`EAR_OVERLOAD_DB` 118 as a sine peak). Fixed in `firmware/src/app.c`: the threshold is capped below the channel's full scale.

## 2. VMID bias buffer (MCP6022 follower, 10 R + 1 uF)

| Op-amp output R | Isolation R | Loop crossover | Phase margin | 1 mA load step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 50 R | 10 R | 1679 kHz | 85 deg | 10.0 mV dip |
| 150 R | 10 R | 631 kHz | 87 deg | 10.0 mV dip |
| 400 R | 10 R | 251 kHz | 86 deg | 10.0 mV dip |
| 150 R | 0.1 R | 106 kHz | 4 deg | 1.6 mV dip |
| 150 R | 47 R | 2371 kHz | 84 deg | 46.2 mV dip |

- Stable with the 10 R isolation resistor over the whole plausible output-resistance range (PM >= 85 deg). Without it the 1 uF load leaves 4 deg: ringing. **Keep the 10 R** (the datasheet asks for a series R above ~60 pF).

## 3. DAC reconstruction + coupling into the amp

| Amp input R | Pass band | Group delay @ 1 kHz | @ 200 Hz |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 k | 16.6 Hz - 8.7 kHz | 27.5 us | 87.7 us |
| 20 k | 8.3 Hz - 8.7 kHz | 26.3 us | 56.3 us |
| 40 k | 4.2 Hz - 8.7 kHz | 25.6 us | 40.5 us |

- Matches the plant model's 2nd-order 9 kHz reconstruction (~25 us at 1 kHz; about 0.8 sample at 32 kHz). The extra delay at 200 Hz is the 1 uF coupling high-pass - a phase lead the identified secondary-path model absorbs, not added latency.

## 4. Amp + driver + earcup: is there enough anti-noise?

The real-noise study needs ~15 Pa peak at 63 Hz for engine rumble (`sim/results/real_audio.md`).

| Driver | Seal | Pa/V @ 63 Hz | Max clean 63 Hz anti-noise | Meets 15 Pa |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bl 0.8 Tm, 32 R | good | 14.9 | 13.4 Pa (116.5 dB peak) | **no** |
| Bl 1.2 Tm, 32 R | good | 22.4 | 20.2 Pa (120.1 dB peak) | yes |
| Bl 1.6 Tm, 32 R | good | 29.8 | 26.9 Pa (122.6 dB peak) | yes |
| Bl 0.8 Tm, 16 R | good | 29.9 | 26.9 Pa (122.6 dB peak) | yes |
| Bl 1.2 Tm, 32 R | leaky | 18.3 | 16.5 Pa (118.3 dB peak) | yes |

- **Driver requirement:** >= 17 Pa/V at 63 Hz in the cup (with the TPA6132A2's 0.9 V peak swing). A weak 32 R driver (Bl 0.8) falls short; a **16 R driver** doubles the pressure per volt from the same amp and supply.
- **Finding - the amp clips before the controller's clamp:** at 0 dB amp gain the amp saturates at 0.9 V, i.e. 7.2 controller units (1/`OUT_PA_PER_V` V per unit), while `y_max` is 10 units (set by the DAC range only). The controller's clip detector never saw amp clipping. Fixed in `firmware/src/app.c`: `y_max` is also capped by the amp swing at the selected gain.
